# What's new in version 2.2.0

## Added

- My YouTube, a new entry in the main menu right below Spotify (Ctrl+Alt+U). Sign in with your Google account and it lists Home (recommended for you), Subscriptions (new videos), Subscribed channels, My playlists, Watch later, Liked videos and your YouTube history. Every list works like search results: Enter plays, more entries load at the end, and the context menu offers download, favorites, playlists and the channel. Escape goes back to the same row of My YouTube. Like every other entry, it can be hidden in Customize main menu.
- Signing in first offers to read your sign-in from the browser chosen in Settings, Cookies, or from your default browser when none is chosen there. If that browser is open, ApricotPlayer has to close it for this and asks first. Otherwise, or when you choose No, ApricotPlayer opens its own browser window with the Google sign-in; it closes by itself as soon as YouTube knows you, and your own browser and its profiles stay untouched. ApricotPlayer counts you as signed in only once YouTube accepts the sign-in, and when YouTube gives the account name, ApricotPlayer says which account is signed in.
- Import YouTube subscriptions in My YouTube adds the channels you follow on YouTube to ApricotPlayer's subscriptions, so their new videos reach the Notification center. Channels already there are not added twice, and nothing is ever removed.
- Sign out of YouTube in My YouTube forgets the sign-in on this computer. Your own browser stays signed in.
- Settings, Cookies has a new choice, Mark played videos as watched on YouTube. When it is on and you are signed in, every YouTube video you play is also added to your YouTube history. It is on by default.
- A Spotify artist page has a new part at the end, About, the way Spotify shows it: monthly listeners, followers, the rank in the world, the cities where people listen, the biography and the artist's links, one per line in a text window with Copy and Back.
- Spotify audiobooks open. Enter on an audiobook lists its chapters, which play like podcast episodes. A chapter your Spotify account has no access to says so, as other unavailable Spotify items do; Spotify sells audiobooks only in some countries.

## Changed

- The setting Show "Resume last playback session" in main menu is gone from Settings, Playback, because Customize main menu already shows or hides that entry. If you had turned it off, the entry stays hidden.
- Checking subscriptions is much faster. ApricotPlayer first reads YouTube's public list of each channel's newest videos, many channels at a time, and asks yt-dlp only about channels that really have new videos. With about 400 subscriptions a check now takes under a minute instead of many minutes, and new videos still arrive with their full details.
- Spotify listening history and Remember keys of played Spotify songs are no longer experimental and are on by default, also if you had them off before; you can still turn them off in the Spotify settings. Turning listening history on no longer asks for confirmation.

## Fixed

- Opening Subscriptions now starts at the top of the list. Before, it could skip the first channels, for example those whose names start with # or a number.
- When a My YouTube list loads, ApricotPlayer now says only "Loading." instead of repeating the list's name.

ApricotPlayer's own subscriptions, history, favorites and playlists stay as they are; the lists of your YouTube account are shown next to them and are read-only.

These changes apply to Windows and macOS. Existing settings and saved data are retained.
