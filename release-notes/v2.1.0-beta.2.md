# What's new in version 2.1.0-beta.2

**2.1 is one of the biggest updates ApricotPlayer has had, and its betas are highly experimental.** If you rely on ApricotPlayer every day, stay on 2.0.3 until 2.1 is released as a stable version. This second beta adds two requested features and fixes what was found in the first one. Everything from 2.1.0-beta.1 is still included; its release notes describe every new command and its keys.

## Added

- Bass boost intensity in Settings, Equalizer: Low, Medium, High or Extreme. Medium is the bass boost you know and stays the default. High and Extreme raise the deep bass much more while the midrange and treble stay as they are. While something plays with bass boost on (F3), you hear the new intensity as soon as you choose it; Save keeps it.
- A Sleep timer button in the player, right after Next. It opens the same Sleep timer as the main menu and Ctrl+Alt+T, which also works in the player. The button says whether the timer runs, for example "Sleep timer: Running, 20:05 left, then playback pauses" or "Not running"; it is read when you reach it and never interrupts you while the time counts down.

## Changed

- The Sleep timer window starts on Current timer, which tells the same state as the button, and Enter there starts the timer. The "Run the sleep timer" checkbox is gone: Start always starts a timer, and while one runs, "Cancel the running timer instead of starting a new one" cancels it.
- Settings, Recording shows the recording format, sample rate, channels, bit rate, folder, whether sources and levels are remembered, and the feedback directly in the section. Save in Settings saves them together with your other settings, without a separate window. The Recording panel still opens the same choices as a form.

## Fixed

- Escape in the player after playing a radio station now goes back to the station list it was played from, with that station selected, instead of the main menu. The same applies to IPTV channels and episodes. As everywhere else, Escape goes back one step.
- On the Mac, closing a list in a form, such as Duration in the Sleep timer, with Escape or a choice no longer leaves ApricotPlayer unreachable for VoiceOver; focus stays on the list.
- On the Mac, the shortcuts with Command+Option+Shift and a digit (Radio, IPTV, Recording, Media controls) and Option+Shift with a digit (quick positions) work.
- Subscribing to a Spotify playlist from its context menu, the sleep timer's finished announcement and the message about the account of a Spotify subscription are now translated into every language.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway. Download only the official release files.
