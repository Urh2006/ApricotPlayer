# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.4

## Fixed

- When focus is in the Player area or on its controls, player shortcuts take priority over conflicting global shortcuts, including custom assignments. This applies to all player actions, including Shuffle and Spotify Like/Unlike. When focus is on main-menu items, normal menu/global shortcuts and letter navigation remain available.
- Spotify Like/Unlike and radio act on the song currently playing when focus is in the Player area, even if a different Spotify row is selected behind it. With focus on a Spotify row, they act on that selected item. Like/Unlike updates the active account's real Spotify library.
- Hide/Unhide can act on the playing song in a known personal mix; its Spotify context restrictions still apply.
- Spotify rows take priority over conflicting global shortcuts for their scoped actions.

## Performance

- Embedded translations are parsed once per language on first use and shared thereafter, avoiding repeated parsing during startup and UI updates.
- Spotify was already present in Customize main menu. Its hiding and the continued availability of Ctrl+Alt+C now have regression coverage.
- YouTube's existing stream cache, next-track prefetch and warm yt-dlp resolver were reviewed and retained; no speculative extra requests or playback fallbacks were added.

## Validation

Automated regressions cover every player action against a conflicting global shortcut, Spotify row priority, the playing-versus-selected Spotify target, and hiding Spotify without disabling its shortcut. Live account mutations and NVDA speech still require user verification.
