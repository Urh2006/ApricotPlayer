# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.10

- Ctrl+A immediately selects the entire Spotify list, including pages not yet displayed. A large Liked Songs collection no longer expands and redraws thousands of native rows just to select everything. Shift+A can exclude or restore an individual song.
- Adding the selection to a playlist reads only the remaining identifiers on a background worker. Spotify still needs these identifiers for the transfer; the interface remains responsive and shows how many have been read. Duplicate handling remains available, and additions use batches of 100 songs.
- Save to Liked Songs applies to the whole marked selection. A selection known to be fully liked offers Remove from Liked Songs with confirmation. A mixed selection offers Save and lets you choose only new songs or all selected songs. Unknown saved states are treated conservatively as Save, never as permission to remove songs.

Mac packages derive their minimum macOS version from the bundled binaries. Current local dependencies require macOS 27.0. The app is ad-hoc signed and not notarized; macOS may require Open Anyway in Privacy & Security after copying it to Applications.

Automated regression tests cover a 13,501-song selection and mixed/all-liked menus. These are not a claim of a live 13,500-song account mutation or complete VoiceOver/NVDA speech verification.
