# What's new in version 1.0.5

## Faster Playback Start and Smooth Seeking

- Fixed: Stream format selection now includes direct high-quality audio fallback on the very first attempt. This removes unnecessary fallback retry cycles and cuts startup latency in half on YouTube videos that do not provide legacy combined video formats.
- Changed: Seeking forward and backward is instant and smooth because audio streams buffer into memory immediately.
- Changed: Highest available standard audio quality (Opus up to 160 kbps and AAC 128 kbps) is always used for fast playback.

## Verification

- Verified startup speed improvements across popular music tracks and videos.
- Confirmed instantaneous seeking forward and backward in mpv.
- Passed all automated test suites, bytecode compilation, and critical Ruff checks.
