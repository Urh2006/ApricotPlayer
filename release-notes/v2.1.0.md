# What's new in version 2.1.0

ApricotPlayer 2.1 is one of the biggest updates ApricotPlayer has had. It adds a 15-band equalizer with advanced settings, audio processing, crossfade, a sleep timer, A-B repeat and saved excerpts, subtitles read aloud, an audiobook mode, a playlist editor, file management, Radio, IPTV, recording, converter options, YouTube search suggestions, media keys, global shortcuts, Spotify playlist subscriptions and notifications for new releases of the artists you follow, settings and bookmark backups, and much more. It was tested in eight betas and three release candidates.

Your settings and saved data are kept, and the shortcuts you already use stay the same. Every new command has its own shortcut, which you can change in Settings, Keyboard shortcuts. The keys below are the Windows defaults. On the Mac, Command takes the place of Ctrl and Option the place of Alt; Settings, Keyboard shortcuts shows every Mac key.

## Added

### Sound

- The equalizer (F4) has 15 bands instead of 10. Advanced sets the frequency and width of every band and adds low and high shelves. You hear changes at once; Save keeps them and Cancel restores the previous sound. Built-in presets can be edited, and Reset this preset brings back the original values.
- Bass boost intensity in Settings, Equalizer: Low, Medium, High or Extreme. Medium is the bass boost you know and stays the default.
- Audio processing (F5 in the player, or Settings, Playback): balance, mono, dynamic normalization with a limiter, silence removal, headphone spatialization with a SOFA file, and more. Alt+Shift+Left and Right move the balance and Alt+Shift+V tells you where it is. Ctrl+M turns silence removal on or off and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H turns headphone spatialization on or off.
- Playback transitions (player context menu or Settings, Playback): crossfade between tracks, off by default, 3 seconds when enabled and up to 30 seconds. Crossfade on Previous and Next is a separate option. Fades on seek, pause, start and end of a file are optional too. A list can also start again from the first item after the last one.
- Ctrl+Alt+Up sets the volume to the highest the player allows, and Ctrl+Alt+Down to 5 percent.

### Sleep timer, excerpts and navigation

- Sleep timer in the main menu, on Ctrl+Alt+T and on its own button in the player, right after Next: stop after a set time, at the end of the track or at the end of the chapter. It pauses by default and keeps your position, can fade out, warns a minute before it finishes, and counts real time or only playing time. Alt+Shift+T tells the remaining time and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T cancels it.
- Alt+L repeats the excerpt marked with [ and ], and seeking stays inside it while it repeats. Alt+B opens Marked excerpts, with exact times in milliseconds, saving an excerpt in Bookmarks and exporting one or all excerpts, with the original sound or with your current audio settings. Alt+Shift+PageUp and PageDown play the previous or next excerpt.
- Ten quick positions per file: Alt+Shift+1 to 0 sets one and Alt+1 to 0 jumps to it.
- Choose how far Left and Right seek: Shift+1 to Shift+0 select 1, 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds or 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 minutes, and Shift+C selects your own amount from Settings, Playback.
- Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9 jump to 10 to 90 percent and Ctrl+Shift+1 to Ctrl+Shift+9 to 15 to 95 percent. On the Mac the 15 to 95 percent jumps use Command+Option with the digit.
- Ctrl+G goes to a track number, Ctrl+Alt+PageUp and PageDown go to the first and last track, and Alt+Shift+I tells the track number, total, played and remaining time. Alt+T, Alt+R, Alt+D, Alt+Shift+P, Alt+N and Alt+I tell the elapsed and remaining time, the duration, the percentage, the title and the artist. Ctrl+T goes to a time and Alt+P to a percentage.
- N says the name of what is playing. Press it again quickly to hear the file path or web address, and a third time to copy it.
- Alt+Shift+L lists everything in the playing list with its numbers, size and times; choose one and Play jumps to it.

### Audio tracks, subtitles and audiobooks

- Alt+A chooses the audio track of a video, and Alt+PageUp and Alt+PageDown switch between tracks.
- Alt+S chooses subtitles and can read them aloud. Alt+Shift+S loads a subtitle file and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F8 loads subtitles from a web address.
- M4B files with chapters open in audiobook mode, and Alt+K switches it on or off for any file. In this mode the chapter keys, the percentage jumps and the time announcements work within the current chapter, while your saved position and bookmarks still cover the whole book. Alt+Shift+C stops at the end of the chapter.
- Tracks of an imported CUE sheet start and end at their own times inside the album file, with their own time, marks and A-B repeat.

### Playlists and files

- The playlist editor (Alt+E on a playlist) adds files, links and folders, imports M3U, M3U8, PLS, XSPF, ASX and CUE, exports M3U8, M3U and JSON, and marks, moves and removes entries. Changes are written only when you save. A folder can stay live, so the playlist always plays what the folder contains.
- Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F2 saves the current playing list as a new playlist.
- In a folder list, Delete moves the selected file to the Recycle Bin or Trash, and Shift+Delete deletes it permanently after you confirm. Alt+Shift+M opens Manage files, which copies, moves and deletes several files at once and asks what to do when a file with the same name already exists.
- While listening, Ctrl+K marks or unmarks the playing file, K tells how many files are marked, and Alt+Shift+K opens Manage files with all of them marked.
- Settings, Playback, "Open a file from the system with": a file you open from File Explorer, Finder or Play file can bring the other files of its folder, or of its folder and subfolders, so Next goes on to the next file.
- Ctrl+Alt+Shift+E lists the recently opened files and folders, and Ctrl+Alt+V opens the file or folder on the clipboard.
- Alt+Shift+F copies the selected or playing file so you can paste it in a folder, Shift+F2 renames it (in Favorites it renames the favorite), Alt+Shift+E shows it in its folder and Alt+Enter opens its properties.

### Radio, IPTV and recording

- Radio (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+1): search stations, browse by country, region, language, tag and more, keep favorites and recently played stations, vote, and open a station's website. The new artist and title are announced automatically, and Alt+Shift+N repeats them. Filters can be set for one list or for all lists.
- IPTV (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+2): M3U playlists from a file or address, Xtream and Stalker logins, live TV, films and series with episodes from Xtream, and the programme guide. Channels say what is on now, and Alt+Shift+N tells the current and next programme. Logins are stored protected. A channel with a login kept in favorites, a playlist or the queue keeps only a reference to its source and channel, never its address or login, and is opened again from the source when it plays.
- Recording (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+3): record microphones, the computer's sound, single applications or everything except one application, with a volume for each source. Ctrl+Alt+F9 starts, Ctrl+Alt+F10 pauses and Ctrl+Alt+F11 stops, and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F10 opens the recordings folder. Recordings are saved as FLAC by default. A recording that stops early, for example when a device is unplugged, is kept as an incomplete recording and you are told where it is. Settings, Recording shows the recording choices directly.
- On Windows, the Radio, IPTV and Recording lists open in the main window. When something plays in the background, Tab goes from the list to the player, and shortcuts work as on every other screen.

### Converter, downloads and YouTube

- The converter converts to MKA and OGA as well. Converter options (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+K or Settings, Downloads) set the bit rate and quality of each format, the sample rate and channels, how many files of a folder convert at once and where a converted folder goes. Conversion can be cancelled with the Cancel button or Ctrl+Alt+Shift+X, and every file that failed is listed with its reason.
- Ctrl+Shift+Down in the search field shows YouTube search suggestions.
- A link to a video inside a playlist asks whether you want only the video or the whole playlist; Settings, Playback can make this choice for you.
- Alt+Shift+D downloads with options: audio or video, format, quality, maximum height and folder for that one download.
- Settings, Downloads: keep the video's chapters in downloads, and remove sponsor, self-promotion and reminder segments with SponsorBlock. Both are off unless you switch them on.
- Settings, General, "yt-dlp update channel": Stable, Nightly or Master.
- Ctrl+B opens the page of the selected or playing item in the browser, and Alt+Shift+B its channel's page.

### Media keys and global shortcuts

- Media keys and the system media controls (the Windows media overlay, Now Playing on the Mac) play, pause, skip and seek while the player is open. Their position slider shows and moves the time of the CUE track or chapter you hear. You can switch them off in Media controls (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+4 or Settings, General).
- Global shortcuts (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+G or Settings, General) work while another program is in front: play or pause, seek, volume, previous and next, and starting, pausing and stopping a recording. On Windows they are Win+Ctrl+Alt with Space, the arrows, Page Up and Page Down, F9, F10 and F11. Each one can be changed or turned off. On the Mac none is set at first, because Control with Option belongs to VoiceOver; type your own in the same window.

### Spotify

- Spotify playlist subscriptions (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+B): follow playlists by other people and hear what changed. By default only added songs are reported; removed songs and order changes can be switched on for each playlist. Checks run every hour by default for the selected Spotify account, and Check now runs one immediately.
- Notifications for new releases of the artists you follow, in the notification center. In Settings, Spotify, "Notifications for new releases of followed artists" opens a list of all your followed artists with a check box for each, loading more as you move down, and the buttons Turn on for all and Turn off for all. Enter on such a notification opens the album. On the Mac this is one switch for all artists for now.

### Backups, settings and announcements

- Export settings (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F6) and Import settings (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F5) save and restore all your settings. Export bookmarks (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F4) and Import bookmarks (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F3) add the bookmarks of a backup to yours; Replace all my bookmarks is a separate choice that asks first and keeps a copy. All four are also in Settings, General.
- Settings, General, "Announcements": Full sentences or Short, for experienced users. Short announcements say only the value where that is clear, such as "40" instead of "Volume: 40", and "On" or "Off" for volume boost and bass boost. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V switches between the two at any time.
- In Settings, F1 explains the setting that has the focus. On a Mac keyboard press fn+F1, unless the F keys work as standard function keys.

## Changed

- Settings, Keyboard shortcuts groups every command in categories, searches all shortcuts and gives each command a primary and an alternate shortcut. A new command never takes a shortcut you already use for something else; it stays unassigned instead.
- The main menu has new entries for Radio, IPTV, Recording and the Sleep timer. The playlist context menu ends with Edit playlist.
- Settings changed and then left with Back or Escape are no longer applied, also a new language, and a playing item goes back to its saved equalizer and sound. After Save, Back keeps what you saved.
- An update is not installed and not downloaded while a recording is running. ApricotPlayer asks you to stop the recording first.

## Fixed

### Screen reader and keyboard

- Pressing Tab onto a list, in the main window or in a dialog, no longer makes the screen reader read the selected item twice.
- Going back to the main menu, or opening a screen with a button, no longer reads the button's new label first. For example, Escape from History used to say "Open" before the main menu.
- Closing a dialog with Escape, for example Bookmarks or Settings, no longer sends you to another program.
- Many translated texts had broken letters, for example "SploĹˇno" instead of "Splošno". They are now shown and read correctly in every language.
- The Bookmarks list, the Action finder list, the main menu and the Settings sections have names for the screen reader, and after closing Bookmarks the main menu is on Bookmarks.
- A notification that arrives while the notification center is open shows at once, the selection stays on the notification you were on, and Enter and Delete act on that one.

### Playback

- Volume, speed, pitch and other sound settings changed while paused apply from the first moment after you press Play, for files, radio, other live streams and Spotify, also when another device paused Spotify.

### Spotify

- Recommendations after a song or an album now go on as long as you listen. Before, they stopped after about 50 songs: at the end of the list Next did nothing, and then the same songs started over. Recommendations you already heard are not repeated, and a request Spotify did not answer is asked again.
- Pause while a song still waits for Spotify stops the sound at once.
- Play on a recommendation in the Spotify queue plays that song, not the first song of the list.
- The last song of a long list is chosen only when the whole list has loaded; otherwise ApricotPlayer tells you that the list is still loading.
- Loading an album or playlist, or a slow answer from Spotify, no longer holds Pause, Next and Seek for a long time.
- Resume last session right after start with a Spotify song waits for Spotify to connect instead of saying that Spotify is not connected.
- Refreshing an open playlist notices changes past its first page when the number of songs changed, and changed edit rights.
- When Spotify refuses to play the songs of an account, ApricotPlayer says so clearly instead of asking you to try the song again. Spotify currently does this for some accounts in every program that plays Spotify the way ApricotPlayer does.
- A sleep timer set to the end of the track knows whether you chose the next song, however long Spotify took to start it.

### Downloads

- Cancelling the folder choice of a queued download keeps it in the queue.
- A batch download in which every item failed is reported as failed, not as finished.
- On the Mac, Cancel also stops FFmpeg when a download is being merged or converted.

## Known limitations

- On the Mac, recording only from a microphone still asks for Screen Recording permission, because macOS gives ApricotPlayer all recording sources through the same system service.
- On the Mac, a form such as Subtitles or Transitions that stays open holds the sleep timer, A-B repeat and the end of a CUE track until it closes. This will be fixed in the next version.

These changes apply to Windows and macOS. The Mac packages for this release are built with GitHub Actions. Existing settings and saved data are retained.

Download **ApricotPlayerSetup.exe** or **ApricotPlayer.zip** for Windows, or **ApricotPlayer-macOS-arm64.dmg** for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 27 or later. The Mac ZIP is used by the in-app updater.

The Mac app is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks its first launch, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, choose **Open Anyway** for ApricotPlayer, and confirm. Use packages from this project's Releases page.
