# What's new in version 1.0.3

## Complete podcast archives

- Podcast and RSS feeds now retain every episode included in the feed instead of discarding everything after the first 500 episodes.
- Press End or move down from the last visible episode to append the next batch without losing your position in the list.
- Settings > Playback > Podcast episodes shown per batch controls whether ApricotPlayer initially shows and then appends 25, 50, 100, 200, or 500 episodes at a time.
- Podcast feeds saved by an earlier version are refreshed once in the background when first opened, allowing older episodes to appear without resubscribing.
- Historical episodes restored by that refresh are not incorrectly announced as newly published episodes.

ApricotPlayer can show every episode that the publisher includes in its RSS or Atom feed. If a podcast provider removes older episodes from the feed itself, the player cannot retrieve those omitted episodes through RSS.

- Added automated coverage for full-feed parsing, incremental loading, legacy archive restoration, notification behavior, and a real wx podcast list.
