# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.24

- New Spotify setting "Remember keys of played Spotify songs", off by default. You find it in the Spotify settings and in the Spotify section of Settings. Spotify lets an account start about 30 new songs in quick succession and then about one new song every 30 seconds, after which ApricotPlayer says that Spotify is limiting new songs. With this setting turned on, a song you have played before starts without waiting for that limit, also after restarting ApricotPlayer. Only songs played with the setting on are remembered.
- The keys are stored encrypted for your user account, so the file shows neither the keys nor which songs you played. At most 250,000 songs are kept, about 18 MB; the oldest go first. Turning the setting off deletes the file.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway.
