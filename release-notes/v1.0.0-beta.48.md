# ApricotPlayer 1.0.0 Beta 48

This beta introduces the first complete AudioVault experience in ApricotPlayer.

- `Search AudioVault` appears directly below YouTube search in the main menu.
- Log in with an AudioVault account or open the exact registration page from the login dialog and Settings.
- Search Movies or TV shows with the same keyboard-first interaction used elsewhere in ApricotPlayer.
- Play movies, open TV-show episode lists, use Enter to play an episode, and navigate episodes with the normal player shortcuts.
- Download movies, individual episodes, or a complete TV show as audio. Video download announces that AudioVault is audio-only.
- TV-show archives are downloaded once, safely validated and extracted, then reused from the AudioVault cache.
- Passwords are not saved, catalog crawling is avoided, and requests are kept within AudioVault's documented fair-use limits.

The ordinary YouTube and local-media playback paths, seek behavior, volume, bass boost, and equalizer processing were not changed.
