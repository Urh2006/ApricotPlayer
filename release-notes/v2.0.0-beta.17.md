# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.17

- While a Spotify song plays, Ctrl+Shift+PageUp plays the first song of its playlist, album or Liked Songs, and Ctrl+Shift+PageDown plays the last song. They follow the list's own order, also when shuffle is on, and also work while you browse other screens with the player in the background. On a YouTube video, Ctrl+Shift+PageDown still plays the next related video. The player does not list these two shortcuts; you can change them in Settings, under keyboard shortcuts. On the Mac they are Command+Shift+PageUp and Command+Shift+PageDown (Fn+Up and Fn+Down on keyboards without Page keys).
- On the Mac, Add to playback queue is now Command+Shift+Return, because Command+Shift+Q opens the macOS log out dialog, and Current downloads is now Command+Option+J, because Command+Option+D hides the Dock. Windows keeps Ctrl+Shift+Q and Ctrl+Alt+D.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway.
