# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.18

- On the Mac, background playback now shows the player itself below other screens, as on Windows. After the screen's controls, Tab reaches Player and then the transport buttons. On Player and on its buttons the player keys work: Left and Right seek, Up and Down change the volume, Space on Player pauses and resumes.
- On the Mac, the VoiceOver cursor can activate list rows. VO+Space on a row opens it, as Return does, and VoiceOver's menu command (VO+Shift+M) opens its context menu. This works in the main menu and in every other list.
- Fixed a rare case where starting something else right after a Spotify song could let Spotify take over the player again with its next song. ApricotPlayer now pauses Spotify before it lets go of it.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway.
