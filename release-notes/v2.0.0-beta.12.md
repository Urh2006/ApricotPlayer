# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.12

- Opening a playlist menu no longer announces an incorrect Unknown saved state error.
- Playlist menus correctly switch between Save to your library and Remove from your library.
- The four choices in My profile now speak their names without misleading empty-section or zero-item messages.
- Preparing the next song in the background no longer gives a false skipped-song message while your current song is playing normally. Spotify may still skip a song it cannot play.

Spotify listening-history reporting is not currently supported. On Repeat and listening statistics may not reflect playback in ApricotPlayer; this version does not change that behavior.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway.
