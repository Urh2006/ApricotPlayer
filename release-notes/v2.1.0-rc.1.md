# What's new in version 2.1.0-rc.1

This is the first release candidate of ApricotPlayer 2.1. It fixes what a fourth full review of the 2.1 betas found, most of all around saved data, recordings and Spotify. Everything from the 2.1 betas is included.

## Changed

- An IPTV channel with a login, saved in favorites, a playlist or the playback queue, no longer keeps its address, login or portal session in your files. Only a reference to its IPTV source and channel is kept, and the channel is opened again from the source when it plays. Entries saved before are cleaned the first time they are read. A playlist exported as M3U leaves such channels out.
- An update is not installed while a recording is running. ApricotPlayer asks you to stop the recording first.
- With short announcements (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V), volume boost and bass boost now say only On or Off.

## Fixed

- Changing the volume, speed or pitch while paused no longer plays a short moment with the old setting when you press Play again.
- Recording: a short stall of the disk no longer loses sound, a microphone that stops while another source keeps recording now gives an incomplete recording instead of a normal one, and Stop or Exit can no longer hang when no source could be opened.
- Exit while the Radio, IPTV or Recording list is open now finishes and saves a running recording properly. On a Mac, Quit while a window like Radio or a form is open also waits for the recording and the playback position to be saved.
- Importing bookmarks refuses a file with a damaged position, instead of importing it as the start of the item.
- Spotify: loading an album or playlist, or a slow answer from Spotify, can no longer hold Pause, Next and Seek for a long time. A song chosen in the Spotify queue no longer triggers a sleep timer set to the end of the song. Refreshing an open playlist notices changes past its first page when the number of songs changed, and changed edit rights.
- On a Mac, Resume last session right after start with a Spotify song now waits for Spotify to connect, as on Windows.
- Shortcuts and the background player's keys now work while the Radio, IPTV or Recording list is open. A shortcut that opens another screen closes the list.
- Back or Escape now closes the Recording list also while a recording is running; the recording goes on.
- Leaving Settings without saving now also drops changed Spotify and Recording settings, and a playing item goes back to its equalizer.
- On Windows, with a dialog open, A-B repeat, the end of a CUE track and the end of a chapter keep their time, also when subtitles or stream details changed meanwhile.
- CUE tracks: marks and A-B repeat no longer carry over to the next track of the same album, and quick positions and Play after the end stay inside the track.
- Cancel of a folder conversion stops only that conversion.
- Radio filters for all lists are now shown in the filter form, so they can be changed and removed again.
- On a Mac, a Radio or IPTV list that loads slowly no longer opens over another screen, and global recording Stop and Pause work while a dialog is open.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway. Download only the official release files.
