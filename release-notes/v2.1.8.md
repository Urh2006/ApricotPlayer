# What's new in version 2.1.8

## Added

- Settings, Playback has a new setting, Playback pitch, right after Playback speed, in the Speed and pitch group. It sets the pitch every item starts with when the player opens, as Playback speed does for the speed, from 0.5 to 2.0. Reset speed and pitch (Ctrl+0) now returns to this pitch instead of always to 1.
- Settings, General has a new command in Tools and backups: Delete all ApricotPlayer data. It first explains what will be deleted: settings, history, favorites, playlists, bookmarks, playback positions, subscriptions, podcasts, notifications, Spotify and AudioVault sign-ins and cookies. No is the default answer, and the warning suggests exporting your settings and bookmarks first. After Yes, ApricotPlayer closes and deletes its data folder, so it starts as a new installation the next time. Downloaded files and music in your own folders are not deleted. On the Mac, ApricotPlayer's Keychain entries are deleted too.

## Changed

- The player no longer offers "Automatically play next item in this player session" while Spotify or a radio station plays. Spotify plays the next song of an album or playlist by itself, and a radio station has no next item, so the choice changed nothing there. For other items it stays as before, shown only when Automatically play next item is off in Settings.

These changes apply to Windows and macOS. Existing settings and saved data are retained.

Download **ApricotPlayerSetup.exe** or **ApricotPlayer.zip** for Windows, or **ApricotPlayer-macOS-arm64.dmg** for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 26 or later. The Mac ZIP is used by the in-app updater.

The Mac app is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks its first launch, open **System Settings > Privacy & Security**, choose **Open Anyway** for ApricotPlayer, and confirm. Use packages from this project's Releases page.
