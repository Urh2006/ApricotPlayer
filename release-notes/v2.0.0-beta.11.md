# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.11

- My profile is now in the Spotify menu, just above Spotify settings. Open it to see who you follow, your followers and your public playlists, or to find friends.
- Add playlists, artists and browsing categories to the Spotify main menu for quicker access. They stay available in their usual places, and you can remove the shortcut whenever you like.
- Playlists and categories in the main menu have the options that fit them. You can play a playlist directly or open a category to browse it.
- Follow or unfollow an artist from search results, a song's menu or the playing song. ApricotPlayer checks the current Spotify state before showing the option.
- Add a playlist to your public Spotify profile or remove it again. This is separate from its Public/Private setting and from shortcuts in ApricotPlayer. The option is available when Spotify reports a supported state for a public playlist in your library.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway.
