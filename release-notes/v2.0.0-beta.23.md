# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.23

- Seeking in Spotify songs works as it did before beta 22 again. Holding Right Arrow or Left Arrow moves smoothly through the song instead of too fast.
- The fix from beta 22 stays: when skipping quickly through a Spotify playlist, ApricotPlayer waits until Spotify allows the next song, says once that Spotify is limiting new songs, and then starts the song by itself.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway.
