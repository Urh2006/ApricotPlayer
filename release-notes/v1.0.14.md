# What's new in version 1.0.14

## Instant Seeking & Resume Position on Long M4A/YouTube Streams

- Fixed: Eliminated freezing, stalls, and playback lockups when seeking immediately after opening or starting long M4A/YouTube streams (2+ hours) by configuring fast keyframe-level seeking (--hr-seek=no, relative/absolute seek modes).
- Fixed: Resolved issue where opening a stream with a late saved resume position (e.g. elapsed 2:23:00) failed to start by preventing linear byte-range decoding over HTTP.

## Verification

- Verified instant position jumping to 2:23:00 on 2-hour YouTube format 140 streams in under 1 second.
- Passed full test suite and compile checks.
