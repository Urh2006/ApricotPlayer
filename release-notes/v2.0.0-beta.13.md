# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.13

- Listening history is an optional, experimental Spotify feature. It is off by default and can be enabled in the Spotify section of Settings or in Spotify settings from the Spotify menu.
- Before enabling it, a warning explains the additional risk to your Spotify account and asks for confirmation. Choosing No or Cancel keeps it off.
- When enabled, it reports only audio played after you turned it on. Pauses, seeking and background preparation do not add listening time. Known Spotify private-session playback is excluded. Turning it off cancels pending reports without interrupting music.
- Fixed a timing problem that could start the next Spotify song instead of the one you selected.
- Rapid Previous/Next changes no longer announce old song failures over the new selection, including when pitch has been changed. Genuine Spotify playback failures are still reported.

The experimental feature uses an unofficial Spotify interface. Spotify controls whether records are accepted and how they affect recommendations, On Repeat or listening statistics. Those changes are not guaranteed or immediate.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway.
