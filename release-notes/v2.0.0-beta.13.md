# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.13

- Spotify playback now sends listening-history records for the music you actually play in ApricotPlayer.
- Pauses, seeking and songs prepared in the background do not add listening time. Private-session playback is not reported when Spotify supplies that state.
- Listening records are sent in the background. A failed submission does not interrupt your music.

This feature uses an experimental, unofficial Spotify interface. Spotify decides whether to accept these records and how they affect recommendations, On Repeat and listening statistics; those changes are not guaranteed or immediate.

The Mac version requires an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 27 or later. After copying ApricotPlayer to Applications, you may need to approve its first launch in System Settings, Privacy & Security, using Open Anyway.
