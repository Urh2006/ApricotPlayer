# What's new in version 2.0.0-beta.1

**Portable version: download it again.** The portable version of ApricotPlayer 1.x cannot update itself to 2.0. Download `ApricotPlayer.zip` from this release and unpack it in place of the old folder. Your settings and data stay where they are. The installed version updates itself as usual.

## ApricotPlayer rewritten in Rust

- ApricotPlayer 2.0 is a complete rewrite of ApricotPlayer 1.0.21 in Rust. It has the same screens, menus, keyboard shortcuts, NVDA and JAWS announcements, and texts in all 27 languages, and the same playback, search, downloads, podcasts, RSS, subscriptions, AudioVault and updates.
- It replaces ApricotPlayer 1.x and keeps your settings, history, favorites, playlists, bookmarks, positions, subscriptions, podcasts and saved AudioVault login. Nothing has to be imported.
- Updates of this beta come from the Beta update channel, which 2.0 selects by itself.

## Spotify (Premium)

- Spotify in the main menu (Ctrl+Alt+C): log in with your browser, several accounts, search, My library, Liked Songs, playlists, Home, Daily Mixes, recently played, your top tracks and artists, browse categories, artist and profile pages, podcasts and episode transcripts.
- Playback through the ApricotPlayer player with all its keys. Next and previous follow the album or playlist and announce "Playing: title by artist".
- Shift+S switches between shuffle off, shuffle on and smart shuffle. Smart shuffle mixes recommended songs into your playlist and announces them as "from smart shuffle".
- Spotify queue (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q), Spotify devices (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+O), liking and hiding songs, adding to and editing playlists.
- Spotify settings are in Settings and in the Spotify menu: streaming quality, volume normalization, what happens when an album or playlist ends, and the order of your library.

## Other changes

- Enter on a YouTube video or a link opens the player at once and says "Playing" only when playback really starts.

## Known limits

- This is a beta. Please report any difference from ApricotPlayer 1.0.21 as a bug.
- Spotify audiobooks are not supported yet.
